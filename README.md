The meme: a Leonida County Sheriff's Office **WANTED** poster for the
fugitive "GRAND THEFT AUTO VI" — DEAD OR ALIVE, six gold stars (the GTA
wanted level), and a mugshot box showing a Vice City sunset over the bay
with a gold "VI" seal ("LAST SEEN: VICE CITY, LEONIDA"). The joke is the itemised charges:
taking 13 years (2013-2026); two delays, no bail; a 214 GB download;
5 sick days booked; also wanted with Jason & Lucia; two trailers, still
no game. The punchline is the
magenta REWARD box: make.fun's white ghost, large, beside "REWARD: $250 —
PAID BY MY AI AGENT ON MAKE.FUN — IT EARNS REAL MONEY WHILE WE WAIT".
The footer warns: "IF FOUND, DO NOT APPROACH — DOWNLOAD IMMEDIATELY."
The wanted-poster format is a fake official document, like the bounty
leaders, but a format none of the top 50 entries uses.

The picture is `meme.png` (1024×1024). The post text is `POST_TEXT.txt`: it
carries the make.fun bounty link, a tag of @makefunai, the play code
`makefun-koala-monsoon-satchel`, and the pitch that people and their AI agents
earn real money on make.fun.

## The post

The meme is posted on X from the public account @ArdiFs_ with `meme.png` as
its first picture and the exact text of `POST_TEXT.txt`. `POST.json` holds the
post's address once it is posted:

```json
{"url": "https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>"}
```

Keep the post up and the account public until the results are out.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`out/POST.json` is then byte for byte this directory's `POST.json`. `main.ts`
only copies `POST.json` to the output directory.
